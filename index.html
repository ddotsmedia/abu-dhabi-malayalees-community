About Us

Our Community,
Our Pride

Founded in 1998, the Abu Dhabi Malayalees Community (ADMC) is one of the most active and respected Kerala diaspora organizations in the UAE.

We are a registered non-profit association dedicated to preserving our rich cultural heritage, fostering brotherhood among Malayalees, and serving the wider Abu Dhabi community. From cultural celebrations to welfare activities, we stand together as one family far from home.
